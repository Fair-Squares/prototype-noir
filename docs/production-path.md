# Fair Squares — Research-Backed Production Path

## Objective

Move Fair Squares from a protocol prototype to a credible, research-backed affordable-housing pilot proposition.

## Website structure

1. **Home** — investor/pilot-oriented thesis: capped-return capital for affordable housing projects the market leaves unfunded.
2. **Research** — CPB/Rijksoverheid/ABF evidence library with a clear implication for Fair Squares after every source.
3. **Pilot** — draft product terms, qualification checklist, pilot KPIs and partner ask.
4. **Fund / Invest / Governance** — protocol application, kept secondary until the legal and pilot structure is clear.

## Public claims and sources

- CPB: broad construction subsidies can have limited effect and can raise land prices; targeted gap-bridging can work when regulated rents make building unprofitable.
- CPB: for constrained middle-income households, more homes matter more than perfect typology; where land is scarce, smaller homes are more efficient.
- CPB: rent regulation creates an affordability/availability trade-off; if rental investors withdraw, supply can fall.
- Rijksoverheid: corporations face a €19.4bn shortfall against €115bn of agreed investments through 2034.
- Rijksoverheid: national target is 900,000 new homes through 2030, two-thirds affordable.
- ABF/Primos: 2025 statistical shortage estimated around 396,000 homes.

## Product discipline

Fair Squares should not present itself as a generic housing fund, subsidy pool or tokenisation-first project. The production product should be:

> capped-return viability-gap capital for specific affordable housing projects, deployed only where the gap is documented and affordability is locked.

## Legal/structural work before launch

Resolve before public fundraising:

- fund vs cooperative vs stichting/STAK vs project SPV vs loan-note model;
- AFM/AIFMD/licensing perimeter;
- retail vs qualified/social investor access;
- consumer/tenant protections;
- tax treatment of capped returns;
- governance rights and covenant enforceability;
- reporting duties and data privacy.

## Pilot KPI set

- affordable units enabled per €1m Fair Squares capital;
- rent discount versus local market or regulatory benchmark;
- affordability years locked;
- project financing gap closed;
- months accelerated versus delayed/cancelled baseline;
- capital mobilised alongside Fair Squares;
- covenant compliance and reporting completeness.
