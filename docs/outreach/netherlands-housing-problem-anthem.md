# Fair Squares Netherlands Housing Problem Anthem

Research date: 2026-05-30

## Core Anthem

> Goede betaalbare woonprojecten lopen vast omdat de businesscase niet sluit, de financieringsmix versnipperd is, en niemand snel genoeg kan zien welk gat door wie moet worden gesloten.

In short:

> Financing is the core pain, speed is the visible frustration, and transparency is the lever that can make financing and speed better.

## What Dutch Housing Organisations Are Really Saying

### 1. Financing is the real wound

For woningcorporaties, the dominant issue is not a lack of dashboards. The problem is that affordable rents, higher interest rates, construction costs, maintenance obligations and sustainability investments create projects that are socially necessary but financially hard to carry.

Use this phrase:

> Hoe maken we de onrendabele top per project inzichtelijk en financierbaar?

Evidence from the outreach research:

- National government reported that housing corporations face a EUR 19.4bn shortfall on a EUR 115bn investment task through 2034.
- Aedes says corporations have many newbuild plans, but financing is becoming the bottleneck.
- SVH / Haaglanden reports a EUR 3.5bn regional shortage, 40% infeasible investment task, and only 32% of required social newbuild expected.

### 2. Speed is the political pain

Municipalities and provinces are judged on delivery. They need homes to move from intention to decision to construction. Zuid-Holland's direct subsidy to Woonbron is a strong signal that public bodies are looking for faster, more targeted routes around stalled projects.

The speed problem is not only permits. It is also:

- too many parties in the capital stack;
- unclear final funding gap;
- slow subsidy and accountability workflows;
- corporations needing certainty before committing;
- wooncooperaties needing land, legal form, bank confidence and member capital before they are investable.

Use this phrase:

> Kan dit project sneller naar besluitvorming omdat de financieringsmix, risico's en betaalbaarheidsafspraken in een keer duidelijk zijn?

Source:

- https://www.zuid-holland.nl/actueel/nieuws/mei-2026/unicum-zuid-holland-rechtstreeks-subsidie/

### 3. Transparency is the wedge, not the headline

Nobody wakes up wanting blockchain transparency. They want trust between parties:

- municipality: where does public money go?
- corporation: what gap remains and who carries which risk?
- bank or funder: is this project finance-ready?
- co-op members: what are we committing to?
- impact investor: what affordability covenant is actually locked?

Fair Squares should pitch transparency as project readiness and shared evidence, not ideology.

Use this phrase:

> Een projectdossier waarin iedereen dezelfde waarheid ziet: budget, financieringsmix, publieke bijdrage, ledeninleg, lening, garanties, betaalbaarheidsafspraken, mijlpalen en verantwoording.

### 4. Greenfield and co-op groups have a different version

For wooncooperaties and greenfield groups, the problem is:

> We hebben draagvlak en een sociaal verhaal, maar we zijn nog niet financierbaar genoeg.

They need:

- a professional finance stack;
- member contribution clarity;
- legal and governance clarity;
- a bank-ready dossier;
- transparent affordability promises;
- trust with municipality and funders.

This is why SVn / Fonds Cooperatief Wonen, Cooplink, CrowdBuilding, RoCoCo and Platform Wooncooperaties Amsterdam are strong outreach targets. They are trying to turn civic energy into financeable projects.

## Best Fair Squares Positioning

Do not lead with:

> Wij tokenizen vastgoed.

Lead with:

> Fair Squares helpt betaalbare woonprojecten financierbaar te maken door de onrendabele top, financieringsmix, betaalbaarheidsafspraken en voortgang transparant vast te leggen in een gedeeld projectdossier.

Sharper version:

> Van maatschappelijk wenselijk naar financierbaar.

## Product Implication

The first Fair Squares pilot should not be sold as a crypto product or even primarily as a transparency product. It should be sold as a practical finance-readiness layer:

- define the project;
- show the onrendabele top / final funding gap;
- map all funding sources;
- clarify affordability commitments;
- expose milestones and accountability;
- give each stakeholder the same project truth.
