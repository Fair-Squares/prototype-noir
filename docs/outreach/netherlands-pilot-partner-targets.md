# Fair Squares Netherlands Pilot Partner Targets

Research date: 2026-05-30

## Executive View

Fair Squares should not lead with "tokenized real estate" in the Dutch housing market. The stronger entry point is:

> A transparent civic-finance layer for affordable housing projects: project-level funding, use-of-funds reporting, affordability commitments, governance rights, and privacy-aware eligibility/compliance.

The most promising first wedge is not a large national platform. It is a small, credible pilot around a real affordability financing gap:

- A housing corporation project with a documented "onrendabele top" or final funding gap.
- A municipality or province experimenting with new subsidy / co-finance instruments.
- A wooncooperatie or community-led housing initiative that needs transparent capital assembly, member governance, and long-term affordability protection.

## Why Now

Dutch housing corporations face a sector-level investment gap. The national government reported in January 2026 that corporations are EUR 19.4bn short on the EUR 115bn National Performance Agreement investment task through 2034, driven by higher rates and maintenance costs. Aedes' 2026 forecast says corporations are planning more newbuild, but financing is becoming the bottleneck because newbuild is largely debt-funded while maintenance and sustainability costs are rising.

At the same time, new financing lanes are appearing:

- Zuid-Holland is now directly subsidising housing corporations for social housing projects.
- SVn is launching the Fonds Cooperatief Wonen with EUR 60.6m allocated to cooperative housing finance.
- Amsterdam, Rotterdam, Tilburg and other cities are actively exploring or supporting wooncooperaties as a "third path" between normal rental and owner-occupied housing.
- Mission-driven platforms such as CrowdBuilding, Cooplink, PWA, RoCoCo and VrijCoop are building the grassroots infrastructure for collective housing.

This creates a good opening for Fair Squares: not as a replacement for public finance, WSW-backed lending, or banks, but as a transparent project layer that makes blended finance easier to trust.

## Recommended Outreach Sequence

1. Start local and concrete: Provincie Zuid-Holland, Woonbron, Gemeente Rotterdam, Woonstad Rotterdam, Havensteder, Hef Wonen.
2. Use the regional pain point: Sociale Verhuurders Haaglanden and Aedes for sector validation and introductions.
3. Build a grassroots track in parallel: RoCoCo, Platform Wooncooperaties Amsterdam, Cooplink, VrijCoop, De Nieuwe Meent, De Walvis, De BinnenHaven and CrowdBuilding.
4. Add funder credibility after the pilot story is clear: SVn, Katalys, DOEN Participaties, Fair Capital Impact Fund, Rabobank, ABP/APG, BPD Woningfonds and Achmea Real Estate.

## Tier 1: Best First Pilot Targets

### Provincie Zuid-Holland

Why it matters:

- Strongest current signal for a live Fair Squares pilot.
- On 18 May 2026, Zuid-Holland announced its first direct subsidy to a housing corporation: Woonbron receives EUR 600,000 for 140 social homes at the Dirck Hoffblok in Rotterdam.
- The province says multiple corporations across Zuid-Holland have applied for the new subsidy.

Fair Squares angle:

- "Digital proof and reporting layer for subsidy-backed affordable housing projects."
- Track public subsidy, co-financing, affordability period, milestones and impact metrics at project level.
- Useful if the province wants to show that direct corporate subsidies are faster, targeted and accountable.

Source:

- https://www.zuid-holland.nl/actueel/nieuws/mei-2026/unicum-zuid-holland-rechtstreeks-subsidie/

### Woonbron

Why it matters:

- First recipient of the new Zuid-Holland direct corporation subsidy.
- The Dirck Hoffblok project in Rotterdam-Delfshaven has a clear before/after: 95 existing homes are being demolished and 140 new social homes will be built.
- Woonbron operates in Rotterdam, Delft, Dordrecht, Nissewaard and Zuidplas, giving it relevance beyond one site.

Fair Squares angle:

- A one-project "deal room" around the Dirck Hoffblok or a similar upcoming subsidy-backed project.
- Show funding stack, public contribution, locked social-rent outcome, project milestones, reporting and community-facing transparency.

Sources:

- https://www.woonbron.nl/dirck-hoffblok
- https://www.woonbron.nl/nieuws/corporaties-haaglanden-regie-en-middelen-nu-nodig-om-wooncrisis-te-keren

### Gemeente Rotterdam + Rotterdam Housing Corporations

Targets:

- Gemeente Rotterdam
- Woonstad Rotterdam
- Havensteder
- Hef Wonen
- Woonbron
- DUWO, Woonzorg Nederland and other parties in the Rotterdam performance agreements

Why it matters:

- Rotterdam's 2026-2027 performance agreements target the start of thousands of rental homes.
- Rotterdam is close to Fair Squares' existing network and has both large corporation projects and a growing wooncooperatie movement.

Fair Squares angle:

- "Rotterdam affordable housing transparency sandbox."
- One project with municipality, corporation, public subsidy and a small capped-return civic capital layer.
- Also consider a second stream with RoCoCo for smaller co-op experiments.

Source:

- https://www.rotterdam.nl/prestatieafspraken-woningcorporaties

### Sociale Verhuurders Haaglanden (SVH)

Why it matters:

- SVH represents 18 housing corporations in Haaglanden.
- Their 2026 signal is severe: EUR 3.5bn regional shortfall, 40% of necessary investments infeasible, and only 32% of required new social production expected.
- This is the clearest documented regional "funding gap" story.

Fair Squares angle:

- Regional project prioritisation and transparent gap-capital dashboard.
- Help corporations and municipalities show which projects are blocked by which gap: land, public subsidy, planning, rent cap, capex, WSW/debt capacity.

Sources:

- https://www.socialeverhuurdershaaglanden.nl/staat-woningcorporaties-haaglanden-op-fronten-grootste-opgaven-nederland/
- https://www.woonbron.nl/nieuws/corporaties-haaglanden-regie-en-middelen-nu-nodig-om-wooncrisis-te-keren

### Aedes

Why it matters:

- National federation and credibility gatekeeper for housing corporations.
- Their 2026 forecast explicitly says financing is becoming the problem.
- Aedes is also working on tools around available resources and financial frameworks.

Fair Squares angle:

- Do not pitch Aedes as a customer first. Pitch them as a review partner and route to 2-3 experimental corporations.
- Use language around "transparent blended finance for onrendabele toppen" rather than crypto, tokenization or DeFi.

Sources:

- https://aedes.nl/aedes-forecast/veel-nieuwbouwplannen-corporaties-financiering-wordt-probleem
- https://aedes.nl/onderhoud-en-verbetering/kans-5-inzicht-de-beschikbare-middelen-en-financiele-kaders

## Tier 2: Greenfield and Change-Oriented Organisations

These are often smaller, less bureaucratic and more aligned with the Fair Squares ethos. They may not have the balance sheet of a corporation, but they are better candidates for co-designing the first believable product.

### RoCoCo: Rotterdamse Coalitie voor WoonCooperaties

Why it matters:

- Rotterdam-specific network for wooncooperaties.
- Started from a 2024 Stadmakerscongres roundtable and now organises events and groups around cooperative housing.
- The second Rotterdamse Dag voor Wooncooperaties is planned for 18 June 2026.

Fair Squares angle:

- Best grassroots Rotterdam entry point.
- Offer a workshop: "How to make a wooncooperatie finance stack legible to members, municipalities and mission investors."
- Use RoCoCo to find one early-stage Rotterdam group with a location or near-location.

Sources:

- https://rococo.coop/over/
- https://rococo.coop/agenda/

### Platform Wooncooperaties Amsterdam (PWA)

Why it matters:

- Formal Amsterdam platform with more than 10, and reportedly 15, wooncooperaties.
- PWA was founded as a formal association in February 2026.
- It explicitly works on knowledge sharing, professionalisation, advocacy and improving the position of co-ops in tenders and real-estate sales.

Fair Squares angle:

- A member-governance and finance-transparency pilot across 1-2 PWA members.
- PWA is a good route into De Nieuwe Meent, De Warren, De Jelt, Eureka and other Amsterdam co-op initiatives.

Sources:

- https://www.pwa.coop/
- https://www.wooncooperatiesamsterdam.org/onze-leden
- https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/357397/wooncooperaties-uit-amsterdam-bundelen-krachten-in-%C3%A9%C3%A9n-samenwerkingsverband

### Cooplink

Why it matters:

- National knowledge network for wooncooperaties.
- Named by the ministry as a partner in scaling cooperative housing alongside Platform31, RVO and De Derde Bouwstroom.
- In 2026 Cooplink is framing the movement as moving beyond the pioneer phase, helped by the national loan fund and solidarity fund.

Fair Squares angle:

- Use Cooplink for discovery: which groups have a location, municipality support and a financing challenge?
- Good partner for a neutral educational session before pitching a specific product.

Sources:

- https://www.cooplink.nl/nieuws/2026-het-jaar-van-de-wooncooperatie/
- https://www.volkshuisvestingnederland.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/01/19/fonds-cooperatief-wonen-gaat-financiering-wooncooperaties-mogelijk-maken

### VrijCoop

Why it matters:

- Dutch adaptation of the Mietshauser Syndikat model.
- Focuses on permanently removing housing from speculation through collective ownership, self-management and solidarity between projects.
- Has a solidarity-fund logic that maps naturally to Fair Squares' capped-return / anti-speculation story.

Fair Squares angle:

- Explore whether Fair Squares can represent the transparent accounting, solidarity contribution and governance layer for collectively owned properties.
- This is ideological fit, but likely needs careful trust building.

Sources:

- https://vrijcoop.org/
- https://vrijcoop.org/hoe-werkt-het-structuur/
- https://vrijcoop.org/ontstaan/

### CrowdBuilding

Why it matters:

- Mission-driven platform for collective self-building and community-led housing.
- Claims 11k users, 95+ locations, 200+ initiatives and 65+ experts.
- DOEN Participaties and Fair Capital Impact Fund invested in CrowdBuilding in 2025 to help scale toward 100,000 collectively built homes in the Netherlands by 2035.

Fair Squares angle:

- Distribution and data partner for greenfield projects.
- The first product could be a "finance readiness" or "capital stack transparency" module for initiatives on CrowdBuilding.

Sources:

- https://crowdbuilding.com/
- https://www.doen.nl/en/nieuws/paving-the-way-for-100-000-community-built-homes-doen-participaties-and-the-fair-capital-impact-fund-invest-in-crowdbuilding/

### De Nieuwe Meent

Why it matters:

- Amsterdam cooperative housing pilot and one of the best-documented finance cases.
- Presents cooperative housing as a non-profit, solidarity-based alternative where residents rent from and govern their own cooperative.
- Its financing stack includes municipality support and a cost-overrun guarantee from Katalys and the cooperative housing movement.

Fair Squares angle:

- Post-financing case study partner: map the financing stack, risk-sharing and member governance into a clean Fair Squares deal-room format.
- Good proof that the sector needs legible, multi-party finance tooling.

Source:

- https://nieuwemeent.nl/financiering/

### De Warren

Why it matters:

- First self-build wooncooperatie in Amsterdam / the Netherlands.
- Financed with GLS Bank plus a participatiefonds and community financing logic.
- Strong narrative value: energy-positive, social and mid-rent mix, high shared-space percentage, resident-led development.

Fair Squares angle:

- Case-study / advisor more than new pilot, since the project is already realised.
- Useful to pressure-test whether Fair Squares' product language works for real pioneers.

Sources:

- https://dewarren.co/archief/2020/12/20/persbericht-we-gaan-bouwen
- https://dewarren.co/faq-1
- https://www.cooplink.nl/initiatief/wooncooperatie-de-warren/

### De Walvis

Why it matters:

- Very strong "greenfield with concrete audience" target.
- Woonvereniging De Walvis is a wooncooperatie founded in 2023 to build 72 affordable rental homes for OLVG healthcare workers in Amsterdam.
- It explicitly works with partners in architecture, financing, care and housing advice.

Fair Squares angle:

- Project-level finance and eligibility tooling: affordable homes for a defined worker group.
- Privacy-preserving eligibility checks could actually matter here: healthcare-worker status, allocation conditions, rent band, member governance.

Source:

- https://dewalvis.co/

### De BinnenHaven

Why it matters:

- Large self-build / cooperative housing candidate with more than 80 members and ambition for 100+ affordable homes.
- Public materials mention a finance structure with roughly 70% bank financing, implying a remaining gap or layered finance need.

Fair Squares angle:

- Strong candidate for a gap-capital and member-governance pilot if they are still pre-financial close.
- Could use transparent member subscriptions, finance stack, milestone reporting and affordability covenant tracking.

Sources:

- https://crowdbuilding.com/
- https://upload.lingacms.nl/tw_744ef01b/Thuis_in_de_wijk_2025/Deelsessie_22_-_Wooncooperatie_De_BinnenHaven.pdf

### WoonAnders Lelystad

Why it matters:

- Greenfield CPO project developing 29 homes in Zuiderhage, Lelystad.
- Strong sustainability story: biobased, nature-inclusive, energy-neutral ambition and community focus.
- Good example outside Amsterdam/Rotterdam, where land availability may be more realistic.

Fair Squares angle:

- A smaller CPO finance transparency pilot.
- Useful if Fair Squares wants a demonstration outside the most politically crowded big-city market.

Sources:

- https://woonanderslelystad.nl/
- https://woonanderslelystad.nl/locatie/

### CPO Nieuwe Gracht, Delft

Why it matters:

- Delft CPO developing two buildings with 29 apartments, shared spaces and a mixed-age group.
- The group converted a 10% ground-cost reservation into a loan from Gemeente Delft, which is exactly the kind of municipal project-finance detail Fair Squares could make legible.

Fair Squares angle:

- Case-study or pilot for transparent municipal loan / member funding / buyer governance reporting.
- Useful nearby Delft/Rotterdam network target.

Sources:

- https://crowdbuilding.com/actueel/verhalen/lessen-over-zelfbouw-van-cpo-nieuwe-gracht
- https://cponieuwegracht.nl/het-proces/

### Woonpioniers / Woonco

Why it matters:

- Design and development support for sustainable collective housing, CPO and biobased building.
- Woonco's Canvas concept combines an adaptive circular building system with an interactive design process for collectives.

Fair Squares angle:

- Product/design partner rather than capital recipient.
- Could help package a repeatable "small collective housing pilot" template: design scope, programme, cost, finance stack and resident governance.

Sources:

- https://www.woonpioniers.nl/
- https://www.woonco.org/canvas

### Gemeente Tilburg

Why it matters:

- In March 2026, Tilburg announced pilot projects for wooncooperaties.
- The municipality frames cooperative living as an addition to the housing supply where residents form an association to build and/or manage homes together.
- This is a clean policy opening for Fair Squares.

Fair Squares angle:

- Offer a lightweight finance-readiness and reporting layer for one of Tilburg's pilot locations.
- Tilburg could be more accessible than Amsterdam, while still having a clear municipal mandate.

Source:

- https://www.tilburg.nl/nieuws/item/tilburg-maakt-ruimte-voor-collectief-wonen-via-cooeperaties/

## Suggested Outreach Messages

Use Dutch for first contact with Dutch municipalities, provinces, housing corporations, wooncooperaties, sector bodies and funds. English is useful for internal Fair Squares notes, but Dutch will sound more credible and less like an imported crypto/proptech pitch.

### For Municipalities and Provinces

Onderwerp: Pilot voor transparante financiering van betaalbare woningbouw

Fair Squares onderzoekt een kleine pilot voor transparante financiering van betaalbare woningbouw: een projectlaag die gemeenten, woningcorporaties, wooncooperaties en maatschappelijke financiers helpt om helder te laten zien waar geld naartoe gaat, welke betaalbaarheidsafspraken gelden en hoe publieke en private bijdragen samen het financieringsgat sluiten.

We zoeken een concreet project waarvan de maatschappelijke waarde duidelijk is, maar waar de financieringsmix moeilijk uit te leggen of rond te krijgen is. De pilot richt zich eerst op transparantie, governance en verantwoording, niet op publieke crypto-speculatie.

### For Housing Corporations

Onderwerp: Projecttransparantie voor betaalbare woningbouw met een financieringsgat

Fair Squares onderzoekt een pilot rond betaalbare woningbouwprojecten met een aantoonbaar financieringsgat of onrendabele top. Het idee is om de financieringsmix, betaalbaarheidsafspraken, subsidievoorwaarden en projectmijlpalen inzichtelijk te maken voor gemeenten, maatschappelijke financiers en interne stakeholders.

We zijn vooral geinteresseerd in projecten waar een kleine laatste laag geduldig kapitaal, betere rapportage of duidelijkere publiek-private cofinanciering kan helpen om realisatie dichterbij te brengen.

### For Wooncooperaties and Greenfield Groups

Onderwerp: Transparante financiering voor wooncooperaties

Fair Squares onderzoekt tooling voor wooncooperaties en collectieve wooninitiatieven: een heldere manier om projectbudget, financieringsmix, ledeninleg, leningen, garanties, betaalbaarheidsafspraken, governance en mijlpalen inzichtelijk te maken.

De eerste pilot moet klein en praktisch zijn. Het doel is om collectieve woningbouw makkelijker financierbaar en betrouwbaarder uitlegbaar te maken, zonder wonen tot een speculatief product te maken.

## Product Hypotheses To Test

1. Can Fair Squares make a project's financing gap legible enough that a municipality, social investor or member group can act faster?
2. Can it track public subsidy, bank debt, member capital, guarantees and capped-return capital without confusing stakeholders?
3. Can it support affordability covenants: rent band, target group, lock-up period, sale/refinance restrictions and clawbacks?
4. Can it produce a useful "project proof pack" for housing corporations, municipalities and co-ops?
5. Can privacy-preserving eligibility checks matter in real cases, such as key-worker housing or member-based allocation?

## Best First Bet

The strongest path is a dual-track pilot:

1. **Institutional track:** Zuid-Holland + Woonbron + Rotterdam around one subsidy-backed social housing project.
2. **Greenfield track:** RoCoCo or PWA plus one wooncooperatie with a location and financing need.

The institutional track gives credibility. The greenfield track gives product freedom and narrative fit.

Fair Squares should use both, but avoid making the first public story about crypto. The first public story should be:

> Better finance infrastructure for homes that should exist, but are hard to fund.
