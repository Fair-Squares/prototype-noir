# Fair Squares — Draft Pilot Term Sheet

**Status:** discussion draft, not legal advice.

## 1. Product

Viability-gap financing for mid-rent / affordable rental housing projects.

Fair Squares provides the final layer of patient project capital when affordable rent requirements make an otherwise good housing project difficult to finance.

## 2. Target project

- Project type: mid-rent / affordable rental new-build, transformation, building split, or rooftop addition.
- Geography: Netherlands, preferably municipality-backed or corporation-backed.
- Stage: land/control and planning path substantially de-risked; financing gap documented.
- Size: initial pilot of 5–50 units, depending on partner and permitting status.

## 3. Capital amount

- Indicative Fair Squares commitment: €250k–€1.5m.
- Use of funds: junior equity, mezzanine, preferred equity, or subordinated project loan.
- Requirement: open-book development budget and clear evidence that the project cannot proceed under the same affordability terms without gap capital.

## 4. Investor economics

- Target return cap: 3–4% net annual return, adjusted for project risk.
- No uncapped upside from rising housing scarcity.
- Distributions paid from project cashflow, refinancing proceeds, or agreed repayment schedule.
- Loss/risk allocation to be negotiated based on senior debt, collateral, partner strength, subsidies, and covenant package.

## 5. Affordability covenant

At least one of:

- Rent capped below local market rent.
- Rent capped against mid-rent regulatory boundaries.
- Income-linked rent formula.
- Allocation to defined middle-income or socially targeted tenant group.

Minimum affordability period: 15–25 years preferred.

## 6. Governance rights

Fair Squares / investor vehicle consent required for:

- Material rent-policy changes.
- Sale of property or project SPV.
- Refinancing that weakens affordability covenants.
- Change of operator or asset manager.
- Budget overruns above agreed threshold.
- Early termination of affordability period.

## 7. Reporting obligations

- Quarterly construction/development update during delivery.
- Annual operating and affordability report after delivery.
- Rent levels vs market benchmark.
- Occupancy and tenant eligibility metrics, where privacy-preserving and legally permitted.
- Use-of-funds and project-level financial reporting.

## 8. Sale / refinancing

- Investor return remains capped.
- Excess proceeds above the cap should flow to affordability reserve, tenant benefit, public partner, or reinvestment pool.
- Early sale/refinancing that breaks affordability commitments triggers clawback or penalty.

## 9. Anti-speculation protections

- Fixed land/control terms before Fair Squares capital commitment.
- No capital used to bid up speculative land price.
- Open-book valuation and development budget.
- Clawback if affordability covenant is reduced or removed.
- Transfer restrictions to mission-aligned buyer/operator where possible.

## 10. Pilot success KPI

Primary KPI: affordable homes enabled per €1m of Fair Squares capital, with rent discount and affordability years locked.

Secondary KPIs:

- Financing gap closed.
- Months saved compared with a delayed or cancelled project.
- Tenant rent burden reduction.
- Private or social capital brought in alongside Fair Squares.
- Covenant compliance and reporting completeness.
